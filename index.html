הסיפור מאחורי KefList

As a parent in Jerusalem, I know the drill: you're scrolling through 15 different WhatsApp groups, trying not to miss the one post about the perfect summer camp or chug for your kid — and half the time you still do. I built KefList because that shouldn't be so hard. One place, every activity, no more group-hopping. Whether you're looking for a summer camp, an after-school chug, or something for the holidays — it's all here.
